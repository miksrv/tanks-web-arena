# Changelog

## 0.1.2

Smarter bots, a round of combat fixes and smoother frames.

### Patch Changes

- Bots: a badly hurt tank falls back to cover once and fights from there instead of running to its base
- Bots: a hull-down target is no longer safe — they shoot at the turret showing over the crest
- Shells no longer pass through a rock to a tank right behind it
- HE bursting on the ground or a wall in front of a tank now damages it with the splash
- Ramming: the double damage and the ribbon go to whoever actually rammed
- Artillery lands where the marker shows at any frame rate
- Shots stay inside the reticle circle
- A tank destroyer with a broken track no longer turns its hull
- Both teams destroyed at the same moment is a draw
- No more frame drops from gun smoke in the sniper view, or a freeze when the result screen appears
- Shadows in the artillery view are drawn where you are looking

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- Links to the game now show a title, a description and a preview picture in chats and social networks

## 0.1.0

First public release: the prototype is live at [tanks.miksoft.pro](https://tanks.miksoft.pro).

### Minor Changes

- Team battles 7 vs 7, USSR vs Germany, 1943: Standard, Encounter and Assault modes with base capture
- A new procedural 900 × 900 m map every battle: five terrain types, eight named maps, weather, seasons
- 28 vehicles in five classes, each with its own armour, gun, speed, view range and camouflage
- World of Tanks style armour: impact angle, normalisation, ricochet, weak zones, AP / APCR / HE shells
- Modules, crew, consumables, fire, ammo rack detonation and ramming
- Spotting with camouflage, bushes, line of sight and shared team vision
- Team bots with roles: scouts, strike groups, tank destroyers and SPGs
- Arcade, sniper and artillery views, penetration-coloured reticle, gun marker, reload ring
- Ribbons, medals, hit log, scoreboard, minimap, post-battle report, team signals
- Procedural sound with stereo panning
- Added this changelog
