## 0.2.0

Two Lend-Lease vehicles, detailed tank models, a voice for every engine and gun, and battle statistics.

### Minor Changes

- New vehicles: M4A2 Sherman medium tank and SU-57 tank destroyer (Lend-Lease, USSR)
- Tanks look new: weathered paint and camouflage, hatches, grilles, tools and tracks that run
- Winter whitewash on vehicles; dust, mud or slush on the lower plates by season
- Sound: every engine and gun has its own voice; nearby tanks are heard, far shots arrive late
- Aiming: a dispersion circle on the gun's line of fire shows where the shell may land
- Light tanks can fire on the move; tank destroyers and SPGs have to stop to aim
- Battle statistics per vehicle in the garage: battles, wins, kills, damage, medals
- Garage: map, mode and weather are picked from cards with a shot of each map
- Weather is a choice of its own: any map can be played in any weather, or a random one
- Garage: vehicles are sorted by class; the last battle report opens as a dialog
- Vehicle panel: a top-down diagram shows broken modules, wounded crew and where hits landed
- Shell tiles show a drawing and the rounds left; the minimap shows only the battlefield
- Leave the battle from the pause screen, or with Enter once knocked out (counts as a defeat)
- The camera stays on your wreck for 4 s before it goes to a teammate
- Small crews: in the T-60, T-70 and Pz II F the commander also lays or loads the gun
- The strike plane (V) is switched off for now

## 0.1.3

Two new tank destroyers, a lake country map and a cleaner battle screen.

### Patch Changes

- New vehicles: ISU-152 and Sturmtiger with a 380 mm rocket mortar that loads only at a halt
- New terrain type, lake country, and the map "Karelia": conifer forests, lakes and rocks
- Every terrain type has its own trees and house style: steppe acacia, mountain larch, coast cypress
- Status panel lists only broken modules and wounded crew; ribbons fade out 7 s after their last event
- Starting places in the line-up are dealt at random every battle
- Bots: a retreating tank drives all the way to cover and fights from there
- Bots: scouts shoot back when spotted up close; a target one shot from death goes first
- Bots do not fire into rocks and hedgehogs, or a rocket mortar round next to a friend
- Small obstacles no longer let a fast shell slip through
- Guns recoil when fired; the sound mute setting is remembered

## 0.1.2

Smarter bots, a round of combat fixes and smoother frames.

### Patch Changes

- Bots: a badly hurt tank falls back to cover once and fights from there instead of running to its base
- Bots: a hull-down target is no longer safe — they shoot at the turret showing over the crest
- Shells no longer pass through a rock to a tank right behind it
- HE bursting on the ground or a wall in front of a tank now damages it with the splash
- Ramming: the double damage and the ribbon go to whoever actually rammed
- Artillery lands where the marker shows at any frame rate
- Shots stay inside the reticle circle
- A tank destroyer with a broken track no longer turns its hull
- Both teams destroyed at the same moment is a draw
- No more frame drops from gun smoke in the sniper view, or a freeze when the result screen appears
- Shadows in the artillery view are drawn where you are looking

## 0.1.0

First public release: the prototype is live at [tanks.miksoft.pro](https://tanks.miksoft.pro).

### Minor Changes

- Team battles 7 vs 7, USSR vs Germany, 1943: Standard, Encounter and Assault modes with base capture
- A new procedural 900 × 900 m map every battle: five terrain types, eight named maps, weather, seasons
- 28 vehicles in five classes, each with its own armour, gun, speed, view range and camouflage
- World of Tanks style armour: impact angle, normalisation, ricochet, weak zones, AP / APCR / HE shells
- Modules, crew, consumables, fire, ammo rack detonation and ramming
- Spotting with camouflage, bushes, line of sight and shared team vision
- Team bots with roles: scouts, strike groups, tank destroyers and SPGs
- Arcade, sniper and artillery views, penetration-coloured reticle, gun marker, reload ring
- Ribbons, medals, hit log, scoreboard, minimap, post-battle report, team signals
- Procedural sound with stereo panning
- Added this changelog
